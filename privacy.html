Privacy Policy

This application is used solely by its developer for personal, non-commercial purposes. It does not collect, process, or share personal data belonging to any third party.

The application accesses only the developer's own TikTok account, via TikTok's official OAuth authorization, to upload video content the developer has created. Access tokens are stored locally on the developer's own device and are never transmitted to or shared with any other party.

No user data is sold, rented, or shared with advertisers or other third parties.
